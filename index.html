Breachpoint terms of use and safety

Version 2026-10-12 · Breachpoint is in pre-alpha

1. Who can play online

You must be at least 13 years old to make an account and play online. If the law where you live sets a higher age for using online services without a parent's permission, you need that permission. Offline play against bots needs no account.

2. Your account

  • No passwords. You sign in with a one-time code or link sent to your email. Codes and links expire after 15 minutes, work once, and a code stops working after 5 wrong tries.
  • Your email is private. It is used only to sign you in and is never shown to other players, not even your friends. You see your own address only partly hidden.
  • Remember me keeps you signed in on that device for 60 days. Only use it on your own device. Without it you are signed out when the browser closes.
  • Never share your sign-in code or link. Nobody from Breachpoint will ever ask for it.
  • You can sign out or delete your account at any time in Options. Deleting removes your account, Name Tag, display name and friends.

3. What is stored, and what is not

  • Stored: your email address, your Name Tag and up to 10 previous Name Tags, your optional display name, your friend code, friends and friend requests, and the date you accepted these terms.
  • For a few minutes only: whether you are online and which match you are in, shown only to accepted friends.
  • Sign-in codes, links and sessions are stored only as one-way hashes, so a leak of the account database could not be used to sign in as you.
  • Feedback and player reports you send are kept for up to a year so they can be reviewed.
  • Your data is never sold or used for advertising. It is processed by Cloudflare (hosting, accounts, relay) and Resend (sign-in emails).
  • Chat messages are not stored anywhere. They exist only in the match.

4. How the game keeps you safe

  • Verified players. Every player in an online match carries a signed match pass from the account server. Nobody can join without an account or pretend to be someone else, and a kicked player cannot rejoin from a new tab.
  • Cheat checks. The host's game checks everything other players send: speed, health, ammo and hits are decided by the host, not by each player's computer. Players are only sent what they could plausibly see.
  • Encrypted connections. Game traffic between players is encrypted, and the site and account server only use secure HTTPS connections.
  • Your IP address. In public matches your connection goes through Cloudflare's relay, so other players cannot see your IP address. In private matches with friends the connection can be direct, which lets the other players' computers see your IP address; turn on Hide my IP in private matches in Options to always use the relay.
  • Chat filter. Offensive words are replaced with *** and links are removed before anyone sees them. Chat is rate-limited and you can turn it off completely.
  • Name Tag filter. Offensive Name Tags are rejected or replaced. Name Tags can change once a day, and friends can see previous ones, so nobody can hide behind a new name.
  • Your controls. From the scoreboard in the pause menu you can mute, block or report any player, and start a vote to kick: a player is removed when 60% of the other players vote yes. Hosts can also approve, kick and ban players in their lobbies, and friends' presence is only visible to accepted friends.
  • Rate limits on sign-in emails, friend requests, chat, reports and feedback stop spam and abuse.

5. Rules

When you play online you agree not to:

  • cheat, hack, use bots, modify the game to gain an advantage, or exploit bugs on purpose;
  • harass, threaten, bully or abuse other players, or post hateful, sexual or violent content;
  • share anyone's personal information, including your own, or ask other players for theirs;
  • impersonate other players or the developer, or pick an offensive Name Tag;
  • spam, advertise, or try to trick other players into visiting links or giving away sign-in codes;
  • attack, overload or try to break into the game's servers, or other players' connections.

6. Enforcement

Reports are reviewed. Players who break the rules can be removed from matches, have their account restricted, or have their account suspended or deleted, without warning in serious cases.

7. Pre-alpha

Breachpoint is an early, free test version made by one developer. Features can change, progress or accounts may be reset, and things will break. The game is provided "as is", without warranties, and the developer is not liable for any loss from using it, to the extent the law allows.

8. Ownership

Breachpoint, its name, logo and original content are made by cbgregg. Artificial intelligence was used to assist with development. Third-party assets are credited in the project's documentation.

9. Changes and contact

If these terms change, you will be asked to read and accept them again before playing online. Questions, problems or requests to delete your data: use Options → About → Send feedback.
